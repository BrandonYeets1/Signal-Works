# Signal Works Core 3.4.10 — Construction Deployment

## Signal Works Construction catalog

Work-zone equipment now has a dedicated creative tab. Existing registry IDs remain unchanged; this is an inventory/catalog move only.

Moved into Construction:
- Cone
- Orange and gray channelizers
- Traffic drum
- Type III barriers
- Concrete barrier
- Traffic rail

## Portable floodlight tower

- Tow-behind generator and four-head tower model
- Right-click starts/stops the generator
- Sneak-right-click deploys or stows the mast
- Right-clicking a stowed tower deploys it and starts the lights
- Deployed lights create a wide level-15 pavement light pool
- Breaking or stowing the tower removes its invisible light emitters

## Portable LED message board

- Three editable lines, 18 characters per line
- Message, left arrow, right arrow, merge-left, merge-right, and caution programs
- Power and deployment controls in the editor
- Live amber LED preview
- Procedural world lettering; no font-render-layer dependency
- E, T, and / remain safe while entering text
- Sneak-right-click provides a quick deploy/stow control

## Assets

No textures were added, replaced, or redistributed. Both new equipment models use Minecraft materials, and the inventory icons render directly from the 3D block models.
