# Signal Works Core 3.4.15 — GTA-Style Sign Templates

## New work only

- Added the supplied square and diamond road-sign templates.
- Square and diamond catalog signs now render as exactly two separated surfaces:
  - front polygon: selected catalog artwork
  - rear polygon: sign-back artwork
- Removed procedural plate thickness from these two sign families.
- Diamond signs use the square template rotated 45 degrees around the sign face only.
- Posts and mounting clamps remain upright and reusable.
- Other sign shapes keep their existing renderer until their own templates are supplied.
- No textures were added, resized, regenerated, or replaced.
