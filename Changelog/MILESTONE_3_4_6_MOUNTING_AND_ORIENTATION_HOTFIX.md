# Signal Works Core 3.4.6 — Mounting and Orientation Hotfix

## New fixes

- Municipal lettering now faces the correct direction on every visible blade face.
- Opposite-side lettering is culled instead of bleeding through and appearing reversed.
- Four-way post signs use tighter independent street/footer bounds so lettering stays inside each blade.
- Hanging signs now move up to the detected mast-arm underside instead of remaining at the placed block's base height.
- Hanging clamps touch the arm and use short straps; backlit cabinets retain deeper hardware.
- Cameras placed against the underside of a mast arm now use a dedicated overhead clamp instead of a wall/pedestal bracket.
- Empty-hand right-click cycles camera tilt.
- Empty-hand sneak-right-click rotates the camera horizontally.
- The Adjuster no longer owns camera angle controls.

## Asset policy

No textures, sounds, or binary models were added or replaced. One JSON camera-bracket model was added.
