# Milestone 2.7.3 — Center Front Mounts

- `horizontal_pole` no longer has an item, recipe, or creative-tab entry.
  The legacy block registration remains so existing alpha test worlds do not lose placed blocks.
- The Stand item now shows the tooltip: **Temporary traffic pole**.
- Signals can be placed one block in front of a mast-arm segment at the same height.
- A short two-pixel US-style standoff, rear plate, and mast clamp connect the signal to the continuous arm behind it (`====[signal]====`).
- Existing overhead drop-pipe mounting remains available.
- No inventory icon PNGs were changed.
