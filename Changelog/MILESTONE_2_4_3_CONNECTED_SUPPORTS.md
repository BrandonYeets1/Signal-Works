# Milestone 2.4.3 — Connected Supports

This visual hotfix makes the modular signal-support parts meet instead of floating apart.

## Changes

- Mast arms now extend eight model pixels behind their placement block, reaching the centre of an adjacent signal pole.
- Small, medium, and large mast arms each include a matching coupling sleeve around the pole connection.
- Standard, two-section, five-section, doghouse, and legacy signal-head models now include a top hanger and mast clamp.
- The hanger height automatically matches the physical height of each signal cabinet, including tall five-section heads.
- Existing world placement and automatic signal timing are unchanged.

## Placement

Place the mast-arm block immediately beside the top pole block, with the arm facing away from the pole. Place a signal head directly below the mast arm. The rear arm extension, coupling sleeve, hanger, and clamp should overlap into one continuous assembly.
