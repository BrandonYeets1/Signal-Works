# Signal Works Core 3.4.7 — LA Realism Pass

## New work in this milestone

- Vehicle signal heads move toward adjacent poles and mast arms for a tighter, flush-mounted profile.
- Side mounts now use realistic twin upper/lower support arms, pole collars, a rear mounting spine, and a compact terminal box.
- Overhead signal heads rise toward the mast underside and use a shorter saddle, nipple, swivel, and housing yoke.
- Adjustable signal arms and legacy mast arms share the same close overhead mounting behavior.
- The pedestrian signal was remodeled as a Los Angeles-style black two-window clamshell housing with larger white-walk and orange-hand indications.
- Legacy center rods were removed from signal-head models; contextual mounting hardware now supplies the support geometry.
- Hanging municipal signs now calculate the true underside of the mast arm, giving standard blades short straps and backlit cabinets compact hangers.
- Hanging traffic cameras now include a full-height overhead saddle and drop stem that reaches the mast-arm block above.

## Assets

No textures, sounds, or binary models were added or replaced. Existing pedestrian icon textures and support materials are reused.
