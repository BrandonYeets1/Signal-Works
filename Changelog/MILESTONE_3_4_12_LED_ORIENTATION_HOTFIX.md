# Signal Works Core 3.4.12 — LED Orientation Hotfix

## New change
- Corrected the portable LED message board's horizontal display orientation.
- Text, arrows, merge programs, and caution patterns now render from left to right on the board's front face.
- The physical message-board model and dimensions were intentionally left unchanged so an updated larger model can replace it independently.

## Asset changes
- No textures, sounds, or models were added, removed, or replaced.
