# Signal Works Core 3.4.3 — Visual Sign Designer

Status: complete and compile-validated for NeoForge 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.240.

## New in 3.4.3

- Replaces the terminal-style municipal sign screen with a visual sign-design workspace.
- Adds a large live preview for both crossing street blades.
- Adds direct palette selection for LA blue, classic green, historic brown, and black/white.
- Adds procedural blade shapes: rectangle, rounded, and clipped corners.
- Adds lettering profiles: standard, condensed, and bold.
- Adds small, medium, and large text-size presets.
- Preserves uppercase, reflective, double-sided, district, block-number, and custom-text controls.
- Prevents the inventory, chat, and command keybindings from closing or stealing focus from the editor.
- Enter applies the current text; Escape remains the deliberate close key.

## Compatibility

Existing municipal signs load as Rectangle / Standard / Medium when the new fields are absent. Existing text, color style, reflection, uppercase, and double-sided settings remain intact.

## Assets

No textures, models, sounds, or other binary assets are included or replaced. All new blade silhouettes and preview elements are rendered procedurally.
